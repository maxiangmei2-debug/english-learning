G1 · 庆祝与社交

MC01 Celebrate!（Celebrations Around the World）★

一、原文

Pinatas In Mexico, people often have pinatas at birthday parties. Parents put chocolates and other sweets inside the pinata and hang it on a tree. Children hit the pinata with a stick. It breaks and the sweets fall out onto the ground.

Noodles In China, people celebrate weddings with an eight-course meal, because the word eight in Chinese sounds like the word for good luck. The last dish of the meal is always noodles. The noodles are long and thin. You have to eat them in one piece - you can’t cut them. In Chinese culture, long noodles are lucky. Long noodles mean you will have a long life.

Mother's Day Many people around the world honor their mothers on Mother's Day. In the UK, Mother's Day is celebrated in March or April. Sons and daughters like to give their mother a day to rest, so they might surprise her by cleaning the house or cooking a nice meal for her. They also send her gifts such as flowers or jewellery. Many families take their mother to a restaurant for lunch or dinner.

New Year's Eve London celebrates New Year's Eve in a big way. Thousands of people go to the River Thames, and then, when it is dark outside, fireworks light up the sky and Big Ben, the bell in London's famous clock tower, chimes at midnight. The next day, people eat a big meal with family and friends, go for a walk or just relax at home.

Coming of Age Day In Japan, people celebrate Coming of Age Day, or Seijin no hi, on the second Monday in January. On this holiday, Japan congratulates people who have turned 20 years old between 2nd April of the past year and 1st April of the current year. In Japanese culture, this is the age when teenagers become adults and take on the responsibilities of being an adult. Young women usually wear a traditional kimono, while young men often wear Western-style suits, and they attend a ceremony in their area. They receive small presents and celebrate with their friends after the ceremony.

三、重点词汇讲解

1. celebrate /ˈselɪbreɪt/ v. 庆祝

  • 语境义：举行活动以纪念某个特别的日子或事件。
  • 原文例句：In China, people celebrate weddings with an eight-course meal…（中国人用八道菜来庆祝婚礼。）
  • 常见搭配：
  • celebrate + 节日/事件：celebrate Christmas / New Year / a birthday
  • celebrate + by doing：We celebrated by having dinner together.
  • 词形：celebration n. 庆祝活动；celebratory adj. 庆祝的

2. honor /ˈɒnə(r)/ v. 尊敬；向……表示敬意

  • 语境义：在母亲节这一天特别地对待母亲，以表达敬意。
  • 原文例句：Many people around the world honor their mothers on Mother's Day.（世界各地许多人在母亲节向母亲表达敬意。）
  • 注意：honor 在英式英语中常拼作 honour，但动词形式 honor 在本文中使用的是美式拼写。
  • 常见搭配：honor sb. / in honor of sb.（为纪念/向……致敬）

3. piñata /pɪˈnjɑːtə/ n. 皮纳塔（墨西哥等国的彩饰玩偶/容器）

  • 语境义：用纸或陶土做的、里面装糖果的小动物造型容器，敲打后糖果会掉出来。
  • 原文例句：In Mexico, people often have piñatas at birthday parties.
  • 小贴士：西班牙语外来词，字母 ñ 表示 /nj/ 音。

4. course /kɔːs/ n.（一道）菜

  • 语境义：宴会中的一道菜。
  • 原文例句：…an eight-course meal（八道菜的一餐）
  • 易混点：course 还可表示"课程、过程、路线"，需根据上下文判断。本文中与 meal 搭配，指"道菜"。
  • 常见搭配：main course 主菜；three-course meal 三道菜的正餐

5. chime /tʃaɪm/ v.（钟）鸣响

  • 语境义：大本钟在午夜敲响。
  • 原文例句：…Big Ben, the bell in London's famous clock tower, chimes at midnight.
  • 常见搭配：chimes at midnight / the clock chimes twelve（钟敲十二点）
  • 词形：chime 也可作名词，表示"钟声"。

6. responsibility /rɪˌspɒnsəˈbɪləti/ n. 责任

  • 语境义：成年人应承担的义务和职责。
  • 原文例句：…take on the responsibilities of being an adult.（承担成年人的责任。）
  • 常见搭配：take on responsibilities 承担责任；have a responsibility to do sth.
  • 词形：responsible adj.；responsibly adv.

7. kimono /kɪˈməʊnəʊ/ n. 和服

  • 语境义：日本传统服饰。
  • 原文例句：Young women usually wear a traditional kimono…
  • 注意：和服本身为日本文化特有词汇，英文中直接用 kimono，无需翻译为 "Japanese dress"。

8. congratulate /kənˈɡrætʃuleɪt/ v. 祝贺

  • 语境义：日本官方或社会对新成年人表示祝贺。
  • 原文例句：…Japan congratulates people who have turned 20 years old…
  • 常见搭配：congratulate sb. on sth.（因某事祝贺某人）
  • 易混点：congratulate 是动词；congratulations 是名词复数，常用于 Congratulations on…!

四、固定搭配与介词（拓展版）

1. have + 活动/物品 + at + 场合

  • 原文：people often have piñatas at birthday parties
  • 用法：表示"在某场合上有某物/进行某活动"。
  • 拓展：have a meeting at the office / have a picnic at the park / have fun at the party
  • 例句：We usually have a big dinner at Spring Festival.（我们春节通常吃顿大餐。）
  • 易混：at 表"点/场合"；in 表"大时段/大地点"（in the park 强调在公园里这片区域，at the park 强调在公园这个场所活动）。

2. put…inside… 把……放进……里面

  • 原文：Parents put chocolates and other sweets inside the piñata…
  • 用法：inside 强调"在内部"。
  • 拓展：put…on…（上面）/ put…under…（下面）/ put…into…（进入，强调动作）/ fill…with…（用……装满）
  • 例句：She put the letter inside the envelope.（她把信装进信封。）

3. hang…on… 把……挂在……上

  • 原文：…and hang it on a tree.
  • 用法：on 表示接触表面。
  • 拓展：hang up（挂断电话/挂起衣物）；hang a picture on the wall
  • 例句：He hung his coat on the back of the chair.
  • 易混：hang 作"悬挂"时过去式/分词均为 hung；hanged 仅用于"绞死"。

4. hit…with… 用……击打……

  • 原文：Children hit the piñata with a stick.
  • 用法：with 引出工具。
  • 拓展：cut with a knife / open with a key / write with a pen
  • 例句：He opened the box with a knife.
  • 易混：hit sb. on the head（部位前用 on，不用 with）。

5. fall out onto… 掉落到……上

  • 原文：…the sweets fall out onto the ground.
  • 用法：fall out 表示"掉出来"；onto 表示"到……上面"，强调动作方向和落点。
  • 拓展：fall off（从……掉落，后接表面）/ fall down（倒下）/ fall into（掉进……里）
  • 例句：The apple fell off the tree onto the grass.
  • 易混：on 静态"在……上"；onto 动态"移动到……上"。

6. celebrate…with… 用……庆祝……

  • 原文：…people celebrate weddings with an eight-course meal…
  • 用法：with 引出庆祝的方式或内容。
  • 拓展：celebrate with fireworks / celebrate with a party / mark with silence
  • 例句：We celebrated the victory with fireworks.

7. sound like 听起来像

  • 原文：…the word eight in Chinese sounds like the word for good luck.
  • 用法：sound 是系动词，like 是介词，后接名词/代词/动名词。
  • 拓展：look like / feel like / smell like / taste like（同类系动词 + like）
  • 例句：That sounds like a good idea.（那听起来像个好主意。）
  • 易混：sound + 形容词（sound great）直接作表语，不加 like。

8. in one piece 完整地；一整条/一整块

  • 原文：You have to eat them in one piece — you can't cut them.
  • 用法：piece 表示"片、段、条"；in one piece 强调不切断、不破碎。
  • 拓展：break into pieces（碎成片）/ a piece of cake（一块蛋糕；口语"小菜一碟"）
  • 例句：The vase arrived in one piece.（花瓶完好无损地送到了。）

9. give sb. a day to rest 给某人一天休息

  • 原文：…give their mother a day to rest…
  • 用法：a day/week/moment to do sth. 表示"用来做某事的一段时间"。
  • 拓展：give sb. a hand（帮忙）/ give sb. a call（打电话）/ give sb. a surprise
  • 例句：I need an hour to finish this report.

10. surprise sb. by doing sth. 通过做某事给某人惊喜

  • 原文：…they might surprise her by cleaning the house or cooking a nice meal for her.
  • 用法：by + doing 表示方式。
  • 拓展：thank sb. by… / punish sb. by… / reward sb. by…
  • 例句：She surprised her parents by learning to cook.

11. such as 例如

  • 原文：…gifts such as flowers or jewellery.
  • 用法：用于举例，后接名词或名词短语。
  • 拓展：for example（可独立成句，后常加逗号）/ for instance / like（口语举例）
  • 例句：I enjoy outdoor sports such as hiking and cycling.
  • 易混：such as 后通常直接跟例子，不用逗号断开；for example 后常用逗号。

12. take sb. to + 地点 + for + 餐名

  • 原文：Many families take their mother to a restaurant for lunch or dinner.
  • 用法：take sb. to… 带某人去某地；for lunch/dinner 表示目的。
  • 拓展：bring sb. to…（带来）/ drive sb. to…（开车送）/ walk sb. to…（陪走）
  • 例句：He took his friend to the airport for a quick goodbye.

13. in a big way 隆重地；大规模地

  • 原文：London celebrates New Year's Eve in a big way.
  • 用法：way 表示"方式"，in a big way 是固定短语，意为"以盛大的方式"。
  • 拓展：in a small way（小规模地）/ in the same way / in a friendly way
  • 例句：The school celebrated its 100th anniversary in a big way.

14. light up 点亮；照亮

  • 原文：…fireworks light up the sky…
  • 用法：light 作动词，up 是副词，构成短语动词。
  • 拓展：light up a room / light up one's face（眉开眼笑）/ burn down（烧光）
  • 例句：The city lights up at night.

15. take on 承担（责任、任务等）

  • 原文：…take on the responsibilities of being an adult.
  • 用法：take on 是多义短语，本文中表示"承担"。
  • 拓展：take on a new look（呈现新面貌）/ take on staff（雇用员工）/ take over（接管）
  • 例句：She took on too much work this semester.

16. attend a ceremony 参加仪式

  • 原文：…they attend a ceremony in their area.
  • 用法：attend 表示"出席、参加"，常用于会议、课程、仪式等正式场合。
  • 拓展：attend a meeting / attend school / attend a lecture
  • 易混：join 强调加入某个组织/活动（join the club）；take part in 强调参与活动（take part in a game）。

五、语法重点

1. 原因状语从句：because 引导

  • 原句（P03）：In China, people celebrate weddings with an eight-course meal, because the word eight in Chinese sounds like the word for good luck.
  • 分析：because 引导原因状语从句，说明"为什么用八道菜庆祝婚礼"。
  • 注意：because 语气强，直接回答 why；since/as 语气较弱，常放句首。

2. 定语从句：who 引导

  • 原句（P06）：…people who have turned 20 years old between 2nd April of the past year and 1st April of the current year.
  • 分析：who 引导定语从句，修饰 people，说明是"哪些"满20岁的人。
  • 迁移：This is the book that changed my life.

3. 时间状语从句：when 引导

  • 原句（P05）：…when it is dark outside, fireworks light up the sky…
  • 分析：when 引导时间状语从句，表示"当……时候"。
  • 注意：主从句动作可同时或先后发生。

4. 并列句：and, or, while 连接

  • 原句（P02）：Parents put chocolates and other sweets inside the piñata and hang it on a tree.
  • 原句（P06）：Young women usually wear a traditional kimono, while young men often wear Western-style suits…
  • 分析：and 表并列/顺承；or 表选择；while 表对比（"而"）。

5. 被动语态

  • 原句（P04）：In the UK, Mother's Day is celebrated in March or April.
  • 分析：Mother's Day 是动作 celebrate 的承受者，用被动语态。
  • 结构：be + 过去分词（is celebrated）。
  • 迁移：The festival is held every year in January.

6. 情态动词：have to / might / can't

  • have to 表示"必须"（客观要求）：P03 You have to eat them in one piece.
  • might 表示"可能"（推测或委婉）：P04 they might surprise her by cleaning the house…
  • can't 表示"不能/不许"：P03 you can't cut them.
  • 注意：have to 强调客观需要；must 强调主观义务或强烈判断。

7. 同位语

  • 原句（P05）：…Big Ben, the bell in London's famous clock tower, chimes at midnight.
  • 分析：the bell in London's famous clock tower 是 Big Ben 的同位语，补充说明 Big Ben 是什么。
  • 迁移：Beijing, the capital of China, has a long history.

8. 主谓一致

  • 原句（P02）：It breaks and the sweets fall out onto the ground.
  • 分析：it 是第三人称单数，动词 breaks 加 -s；sweets 是复数，动词 fall 不加 -s。
  • 规则：一般现在时中，第三人称单数主语动词加 -s/-es。

9. 一般现在时表习惯性/经常性动作

  • 全篇基调：文章使用大量一般现在时，说明这些是可重复的风俗习惯。
  • 例句（P02）：people often have piñatas at birthday parties.
  • 标志词：often, usually, always。

六、常用句型与表达迁移

句型 1：In + 国家, people + often/usually + 动词 + 节日/活动.

  • 原文（P02）：In Mexico, people often have piñatas at birthday parties.
  • 功能：介绍某地习俗。
  • 迁移仿写：
  • In Spain, people usually eat twelve grapes at midnight on New Year's Eve.
  • In Korea, people often wear hanbok during the Lunar New Year.

句型 2：…celebrate… with…, because…

  • 原文（P03）：In China, people celebrate weddings with an eight-course meal, because the word eight in Chinese sounds like the word for good luck.
  • 功能：说明庆祝方式及其原因。
  • 迁移仿写：In Japan, families celebrate Children's Day with koinobori, because the carp symbolizes strength and success.

句型 3：In + 文化, … is/are + 形容词/象征意义. … means…

  • 原文（P03）：In Chinese culture, long noodles are lucky. Long noodles mean you will have a long life.
  • 功能：解释某事物的文化象征。
  • 迁移仿写：In Western culture, red roses are romantic. They mean love and passion.

句型 4：…like to give sb. a + 时间 + to rest, so they might + 动词…

  • 原文（P04）：Sons and daughters like to give their mother a day to rest, so they might surprise her by cleaning the house or cooking a nice meal for her.
  • 功能：表达"让某人休息，并通过某种方式表达心意"。
  • 迁移仿写：On Father's Day, children like to give their father a day to rest, so they might take him fishing or cook his favorite dish.

句型 5：…, when + 时间状语从句, … + and…

  • 原文（P05）：Thousands of people go to the River Thames, and then, when it is dark outside, fireworks light up the sky and Big Ben… chimes at midnight.
  • 功能：按时间顺序描述事件发展。
  • 迁移仿写：Many people gather in the square, and then, when the clock strikes twelve, fireworks light up the sky and everyone cheers.

句型 6：On this holiday, + 国家/地方 + congratulates + people who + 定语从句.

  • 原文（P06）：On this holiday, Japan congratulates people who have turned 20 years old between 2nd April of the past year and 1st April of the current year.
  • 功能：说明某节日庆祝的对象。
  • 迁移仿写：On this day, the school honors students who have made great progress this term.

句型 7：A + usually/often + …, while B + usually/often + …

  • 原文（P06）：Young women usually wear a traditional kimono, while young men often wear Western-style suits…
  • 功能：对比两类人或事物的不同做法。
  • 迁移仿写：In winter, people in the north often wear thick coats, while people in the south may still wear light jackets.

七、易错点提醒

  1. **honor vs. honour：美式拼写 honor，英式 honour，本文使用美式。
  2. jewellery：英式拼写；美式常作 jewelry。本文使用英式。
  3. the word for good luck：不是 "the word of good luck"，要用 for 表示"对应……的词"。
  4. between… and…：between 2nd April of the past year and 1st April of the current year，固定搭配。
  5. in the UK / in China / in Japan / in Mexico：国家前用 in。
  6. on Mother's Day / on New Year's Eve / on this holiday：具体某一天用 on。
  7. at midnight / at birthday parties：时刻/场合用 at。

八、一句话总结

本文用平实的一般现在时和并列结构，介绍了五种来自不同文化的庆祝方式，重点展示了食物、礼物、仪式和象征意义在节日中的表达。

九、补遗：正章未充分展开的重点介词与短语

1. at midnight 在午夜

  • 原文（P05）：…Big Ben, the bell in London's famous clock tower, chimes at midnight.
  • 规则：at 用于「时刻/一天中的某个节点」，尤其是固定表达：at midnight（午夜）、at noon（正午）、at dawn（黎明）、at night（夜里）、at 12 o'clock。
  • 对比：in the morning / in the evening（较长时段用 in）；on Monday morning / on the night of April 1st（具体某天的某时段用 on）。
  • 迁移：The party ends at midnight.

2. of the past year / of the current year ……的（表所属/参照范围）

  • 原文（P06）：…people who have turned 20 years old between 2nd April of the past year and 1st April of the current year.
  • 规则：of 表示「属于/参照某一范围」，构成 "名词 + of + 时间范围" 的定语结构。
  • 同类：the first day of the month / the last dish of the meal / the responsibilities of being an adult（of + 动名词）。
  • 注意：of 核心义是「……的（所属、部分、属性、来源）」，不能随意替换 's。

3. in Chinese / in Chinese culture / in Japanese culture 用中文 / 在……文化中

  • 原文（P03/P06）：the word eight in Chinese…；in Chinese culture, long noodles are lucky.；in Japanese culture, this is the age…
  • 规则：in + 语言（用某种语言）；in + 文化/领域（在……文化背景下）。
  • 对比：with Chinese 语义不同，本文用 in 才准确。

4. around the world 全世界

  • 原文（P04）：Many people around the world honor their mothers…
  • 规则：around = throughout；around the world = all over the world = worldwide。
  • 迁移：This custom is popular around the world.

5. thousands of 成千上万的

  • 原文（P05）：…thousands of people go to the River Thames…
  • 规则：具体数字后不加 of（two thousand people）；模糊数量 + of（thousands of people）。hundred / million / billion 同理。
  • 迁移：Millions of viewers watched the show.

6. go to + 地点 / go for a walk 去某地 / 去散步

  • 原文（P05）：go to the River Thames…；go for a walk or just relax at home.
  • 规则：go to + 名词性地点（go to the river / go to school，但 go home 不加 to）；go for a walk / go for a swim（go for + 名词表活动）。
  • 对比：take a walk（散步，强调动作本身）vs. go for a walk（去散步，强调出门去做）。

7. at home / in their area 在家 / 在某人所在区域

  • 原文（P05/P06）：relax at home.；attend a ceremony in their area.
  • 规则：at home 固定搭配，home 前无冠词；in their area 中 in 表较大地点范围。
  • 对比：at 用于较小/点状地点（at home, at the station），in 用于有边界区域（in the city, in their area）。

8. after the ceremony 仪式之后

  • 原文（P06）：…celebrate with their friends after the ceremony.
  • 规则：after 是时间介词，后接名词或从句。
  • 同类：before the meal / during the festival / until midnight。

9. for 的多种含义（集中辨析）

文中 for 出现多次，含义各异： - 表对象/受益：cooking a nice meal for her；take their mother to a restaurant for lunch or dinner - 表「对应/关于」：the word for good luck - 表目的：go for a walk - 规则：for 是英语里最"万能"的介词之一，不能一律译成「为了」。

10. on the second Monday in January 一月的第二个周一

  • 原文（P06）：…on the second Monday in January.
  • 规则：具体某一天/带 day/Monday/date 用 on；月份、年份、季节用 in。
  • 迁移：on the first day of the new year in 2026。

小结：本节补全了 at midnight、of 表所属/参照、in + 语言/文化、around the world、thousands of、go to/for、at home/in their area、after、for 的多义，以及 on/in 的时间层级。
